## 2. Repository Investigation & Architecture

### 2.1 Existing Layout & Sidebar Conventions

The Kynergy Intelligence module incorporates a responsive, collapsible sidebar layout:

- **Desktop:** A fixed 72-pixel (`w-72`) sidebar embedded within [`chat-workspace.tsx`](file:///c:/Projects/kinergy-platform/apps/web/src/modules/intelligence/assistant/components/chat-workspace.tsx).
- **Mobile:** A slide-over sheet drawer triggered by the header menu button and auto-closed upon conversation selection or new conversation creation.

- **Item Summary:** Displays conversation title, turn counter (`X turnos`), and formatted relative/calendar date with a clock icon.

### 2.2 Date Grouping Architecture

To provide clear temporal context while preserving authoritative server-side pagination and ordering, date grouping was decoupled into a pure utility:

- **Location:** [`apps/web/src/modules/intelligence/assistant/utils/conversation-grouping.util.ts`](file:///c:/Projects/kinergy-platform/apps/web/src/modules/intelligence/assistant/utils/conversation-grouping.util.ts)
- **Bucket Definitions:**
  - **Hoy:** Timestamps on or after the start of today (`00:00:00.000` local time).
  - **Ayer:** Timestamps on or after the start of yesterday, before today.
  - **Últimos 7 días:** Timestamps within the last 7 calendar days, before yesterday.
  - **Anteriores:** Timestamps older than 7 calendar days.
- **Bucket Invariant:** Empty buckets are excluded from the returned array, preventing empty group headers. The original array order within each group is strictly maintained.

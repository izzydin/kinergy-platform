## 1. Executive Summary

Milestone **AI-10.1-05** establishes consistent, reusable, domain-driven resource-level authorization policies across all AI entities: **Knowledge Documents**, **Document Versions**, **AI Conversations**, **Conversation Turns**, **Messages**, and **Citations**.

Building upon the canonical AI permission catalog established in AI-10.1-04, this milestone centralizes resource ownership checks into a transport-independent domain service (`AiResourceAuthorizationPolicy`) within `@kinergy-platform/core`. This eliminates fragmented, duplicated controller-level authorization checks, prevents Broken Object-Level Authorization (BOLA / IDOR), enforces anti-enumeration through strict 404 responses for unauthorized conversation access, ensures version inheritance of visibility clearance, and enforces dynamic revocation for knowledge sources and citation preview tokens.

All unit and integration test suites passed with 100% success rate (50 new tests across 2 dedicated suites, 325 passing tests in core, 143 passing tests in api), with zero TypeScript compilation errors.

## 3. Section A: Document Authorization Implementation

### 3.1 Authoritative Owner and Multi-Tenant Scope

Every knowledge document is rooted in `KnowledgeDocument`:

- **Authoritative Creator:** Tracked strictly in `KnowledgeDocument.uploadedBy` (extracted directly from authenticated principal `actor.actorId`).
- **Tenant Scope:** Tracked strictly in `KnowledgeDocument.tenantId` (derived from trusted JWT claims).
- **Physical Partitioning:** Database repositories and domain policies reject any operation where `doc.tenantId !== actor.tenantId`.

### 3.2 Reusable Policy Checks

The policy `AiResourceAuthorizationPolicy` exposes:

```typescript
canAccessDocument(actor: AiActorContext, doc: KnowledgeDocument, allowedScopes?: string[]): boolean
assertDocumentAccess(actor: AiActorContext, doc: KnowledgeDocument, allowedScopes?: string[]): void
canManageDocument(actor: AiActorContext, doc: KnowledgeDocument): boolean
assertDocumentManage(actor: AiActorContext, doc: KnowledgeDocument): void
```

- **Management Clearance:** Evaluates whether `doc.uploadedBy === actor.actorId`, or whether the user holds `ai.documents.manage` or administrative role (`Owner`, `Admin`, `Manager`).
- **Scope Clearance:** Matches `doc.visibilityScope` against the actor's authorized scopes (`allowedScopes` from `actor.visibilityScopes` or role defaults).

### 3.3 Version Lineage Inheritance

Document versions (`DocumentVersion`) do not maintain independent or conflicting access policies. Policy method `assertDocumentVersionAccess`:

1. Validates parent-child lineage: `version.documentId === parentDoc.id`.
2. Validates parent document access: `assertDocumentAccess(actor, parentDoc, allowedScopes)`.
3. If the parent document is soft-deleted or its visibility scope is restricted, version retrieval is immediately blocked.

### 3.4 Operational Uniformity and Lifecycle Management

All operational lifecycle endpoints utilize identical ownership policies:

- **Metadata Updates (`PATCH /api/v1/ai/documents/:id`)**: Enforces `assertDocumentManage`.
- **Soft Deletion (`DELETE /api/v1/ai/documents/:id`)**: Enforces `assertDocumentManage`. Sets `deletedAt = new Date()`, setting status to `DELETED`.
- **Restoration (`POST /api/v1/ai/documents/:id/restore`)**: Restricted exclusively to users holding `ai.documents.manage` (Admin/Owner/Manager). Normal uploaders cannot restore soft-deleted documents without administrative privilege.
- **Ingestion Retries (`POST /api/v1/ai/documents/:id/retry`)**: Enforces `assertDocumentManage`.
- **Version Uploads (`POST /api/v1/ai/documents/:id/versions`)**: Enforces `assertDocumentManage`.

### 3.5 Anti-Enumeration & Fail-Closed Status Codes

To prevent attackers from enumerating document IDs across tenants or verifying the existence of soft-deleted records:

- **Cross-Tenant or Soft-Deleted Access:** Throws `DocumentNotFoundException` ($404$). An attacker probing an unowned tenant or deleted document receives the exact same response as a non-existent UUID.
- **Tenant-Match Scope Clearance Failure:** Throws `UnauthorizedDocumentAccessException` ($403$). The caller is informed that their clinical/operational role lacks sufficient visibility clearance.

## 4. Section B: Conversation Authorization Implementation

### 4.1 Authoritative Owner and Tenant Scope

Every conversation session is rooted in `AiConversation`:

- **Authoritative Owner:** Recorded in `AiConversation.userId`. Client-supplied user identifiers in payloads are strictly rejected; the backend sets `userId = actor.actorId`.
- **Tenant Scope:** Recorded in `AiConversation.tenantId`.

### 4.2 Anti-BOLA Protection via Non-Enumerating 404

Broken Object-Level Authorization (BOLA / IDOR) is systematically prevented by `assertConversationAccess`:

```typescript
if (conversation.tenantId !== actor.tenantId || conversation.userId !== actor.actorId) {
  throw new ConversationNotFoundError(conversation.id);
}
```

If a clinician or staff member probes a conversation owned by a colleague within the same clinic/tenant, the system throws `ConversationNotFoundError`, resulting in an HTTP $404\text{ Not Found}$. The existence of a colleague's conversation is never leaked.

### 4.3 Administrative Archival and Inspection

- **Archival (`DELETE /api/v1/ai/conversations/:id`):** Permitted to the conversation creator. Non-creators may only archive if they possess `ai.conversations.manage` or an administrative role (`Owner`, `Admin`), enforced via `assertConversationArchival`.
- **Clinical Audit Inspection (`POST /api/v1/ai/conversations/:id/audit-inspect`):** Restricted strictly to tenant `Owner` via `@Roles('Owner')` and permission `ai.conversations.audit`. Requires a mandatory clinical audit justification ($\ge 10$ characters) which is permanently recorded in the audit trail.

### 4.4 Hierarchical Binding of Turns, Messages, and Citations

Sub-resources can never be accessed in isolation or cross-referenced:

- **Message Access:** Method `assertMessageBelongsToConversation(message.conversationId, expectedConvId, msgId)` ensures messages cannot be referenced under a different conversation.
- **Citation Access:** Method `assertCitationBelongsToMessage(citation.messageId, expectedMsgId, citationId, convId)` verifies citation lineage.
- **Generation & Regeneration:** Turn creation and turn regeneration (`POST /.../turns/:turnId/regenerate`) assert parent conversation ownership before dispatching to the BullMQ worker.

## 5. Section C: Knowledge Source Authorization Implementation

### 5.1 Dynamic Document Eligibility Rules

A document is eligible for inclusion in semantic retrieval and RAG generation if and only if `isDocumentEligibleAsKnowledgeSource(doc, actorTenantId, allowedScopes)` returns `true`:

1. `doc.tenantId === actorTenantId` (Strict tenant isolation).
2. `doc.deletedAt == null` (Excludes soft-deleted documents).
3. `doc.status === 'INDEXED'` (Document processing and embedding must be complete).
4. `allowedScopes.includes(doc.visibilityScope)` (User must hold visibility clearance for the document's classification tier).

### 5.2 Pre-Retrieval SQL Filtering in pgvector

In `RetrievalSecurityPolicyService.buildRetrievalFilter`, the search filters passed to pgvector embeddings queries are constructed strictly from authoritative actor attributes:

```typescript
return {
  tenantId: actor.tenantId,
  allowedScopes: actor.visibilityScopes,
  isDeleted: false,
};
```

Unclassified or higher-clearance chunks are filtered out at the SQL query planner level, preventing unauthorized context from ever reaching the prompt assembly pipeline.

### 5.3 Request-Time Citation & Preview Re-evaluation

While historical citations stored on messages record the snapshot of source metadata used during generation, live document access and preview tokens are dynamically re-evaluated:

1. **Dynamic Revocation:** If a document is soft-deleted, modified, or has its visibility scope restricted after a conversation was generated, subsequent requests to `POST /api/v1/ai/citations/preview-token` re-evaluate `assertCitationSourceAccess`. Access is immediately rejected ($404$ or $403$).
2. **HMAC Cryptographic Preview Tokens:** Preview tokens generated by `PreviewTokenService` embed:
   - `tenantId`
   - `documentId`
   - `pageNumber`
   - `expiresAt` (5-minute TTL)
   - Cryptographic HMAC-SHA256 signature
3. **Preview Consumption:** `GET /api/v1/ai/citations/preview` validates the signature, verifies expiration, and re-confirms tenant isolation before delivering the preview content.

## 6. Section D: Reusable Policy Architecture & Affected Components

### 6.1 Architecture Overview

The resource authorization logic is centralized in:

- `packages/core/src/ai/application/services/ai-resource-authorization.policy.ts`

This service is purely domain-driven and framework-independent. It does not import `@nestjs/common`, Express, or HTTP response objects, allowing it to be utilized identically in HTTP controllers, domain use cases, and background asynchronous queue workers (`ai-turn-generation.worker.ts`).

### 6.2 Affected Domain Entities & Ports

- **Entities:**
  - `KnowledgeDocument`: `uploadedBy`, `tenantId`, `visibilityScope`, `deletedAt`, `status`.
  - `DocumentVersion`: `documentId`, `versionNumber`, `storageKey`.
  - `AiConversation`: `userId`, `tenantId`, `isArchived`.
  - `AiConversationTurn`: `conversationId`, `turnNumber`.
  - `AiConversationMessage`: `conversationId`, `turnId`, `role`.
  - `AiConversationCitation`: `messageId`, `documentId`.
- **Repository Ports:**
  - `IKnowledgeDocumentRepositoryPort`: Requires tenant-scoped queries with soft-delete exclusion (`deletedAt IS NULL`).
  - `IAiConversationRepositoryPort`: Enforces `tenantId` and `userId` filters on all listing and find operations.
  - `IAiConversationMessageRepositoryPort`: Enforces query filtering by `conversationId`.
  - `IAiConversationCitationRepositoryPort`: Enforces query filtering by `messageId`.

### 6.3 Integrated Application Services & Controllers

- `AiResourceAuthorizationPolicy`: Centralized policy enforcement engine.
- `RetrievalSecurityPolicyService`: Implements pre-retrieval filtering and dynamic document eligibility checks.
- `AiDocumentController`: Replaces ad-hoc ownership logic with `resourcePolicy.assertDocumentManage` and `resourcePolicy.canManageDocument`.
- `AiConversationController`: Enforces `assertConversationAccess` and `assertConversationArchival`.
- `PreviewTokenService`: Leverages `assertCitationSourceAccess` for preview token issuance.

## 7. Section E: Ownership Model Limitations & Future Roadmap

1. **Single Uploader vs. Collaborative Document Management:**
   - _Current Implementation:_ Documents have a single `uploadedBy` user ID. Non-uploaders cannot edit document metadata or upload revisions unless they hold the broad `ai.documents.manage` permission.
   - _Future Enhancement:_ Support co-ownership or department-level document stewardship (e.g., assigning ownership to a clinical department or user group).
2. **Clinician Delegation and Handoff:**
   - _Current Implementation:_ Conversations are strictly bound to the creator's user UUID. Clinicians covering for colleagues on leave cannot inspect or continue peer conversations.
   - _Future Enhancement:_ Introduce formal clinical handoff capabilities with explicit access delegation grants and audit logging.
3. **Granular Team Scoping:**
   - _Current Implementation:_ Tenancy is currently single-tenant per clinic with role-based visibility scopes (`ALL_STAFF`, `CLINICAL_STAFF`).
   - _Future Enhancement:_ Support multi-clinic conglomerates with cross-facility branch isolation.

## 10. Conclusion & AI-10.1 Readiness Verdict

Milestone **AI-10.1-05** completes the implementation of resource-level authorization for the Kynergy Intelligence platform.

- **Document security** enforces strict multi-tenant boundary isolation, uploader-based management, parent-version clearance inheritance, and non-enumerating 404 responses for deleted or unowned documents.
- **Conversation security** guarantees that clinical and staff conversations cannot be probed or intercepted (anti-BOLA 404), with hierarchical integrity linking turns, messages, and citations.
- **Knowledge source security** dynamically validates document eligibility and visibility clearance at query time and preview time, instantly revoking access upon document deletion or role change.
- **Clean Architecture** is preserved with `AiResourceAuthorizationPolicy` providing a single source of truth for authorization invariants across all transport and background contexts.

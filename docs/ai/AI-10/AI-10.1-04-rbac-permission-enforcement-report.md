## 1. Executive Summary

Milestone **AI-10.1-04** integrates artificial intelligence capabilities into Kynergy's existing Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) architecture. In accordance with the approved authorization policy (`AI-10.1-AUTHORIZATION-POLICY.md`) and Architectural Decision Record (`ADR-AI-10-003`), six canonical dot-notation permission codes have been standardized under `PERMISSIONS.AI`.

Ad-hoc role checks in controllers have been replaced by declarative `@RequirePermissions()` decorators evaluated by NestJS `AuthorizationGuard` and `DefaultAuthorizationEvaluator`. Resource-level authorization invariants (anti-BOLA and anti-IDOR) are preserved; general permissions never bypass resource ownership rules without explicit, narrow administrative authority.

All 11 test suites covering 156 unit and integration tests passed cleanly, and `tsc --noEmit` passed with zero errors.

## 4. Role Mapping Breakdown (`SYSTEM_ROLE_PERMISSIONS`)

### 1. `Owner` (Tenant Administrator)

- Receives all 40 system permissions (`ALL_PERMISSION_CODES`), including all 6 AI permissions.
- Exclusively authorized to execute `POST /ai/conversations/:id/audit-inspect` via `@Roles('Owner')`.

### 2. `Therapist` / `Kinesiologist` (Clinical Tier)

- Receives:
  - `ai.conversations.create`
  - `ai.documents.upload`
  - `ai.knowledge.query`
- Clearance: Clinical knowledge bases (`CLINICAL_STAFF`, `ALL_STAFF`).
- Restrictions: Cannot manage or audit peer conversations or view tenant-wide ingestion queues.

### 3. `Trainer` (Operational Staff Tier)

- Receives:
  - `ai.conversations.create`
  - `ai.documents.upload`
  - `ai.knowledge.query`
- Clearance: Operational knowledge bases (`ALL_STAFF`).
- Restrictions: Cannot access clinical knowledge bases (`CLINICAL_STAFF`), administrative ingestion queues, or peer conversations.

### 4. `Receptionist` & `Kitchen Staff` (General Staff Tier)

- Receives:
  - `ai.conversations.create`
  - `ai.knowledge.query`
- Clearance: General knowledge bases (`ALL_STAFF`).
- Restrictions: Cannot upload knowledge documents (`ai.documents.upload`), access administrative ingestion queues (`ai.documents.manage`), or access peer conversations.

## 5. Security Invariants & Fail-Closed Safeguards

1. **Client Payload Tampering Invariant:**  
   Clients passing arbitrary `roles` or `permissions` in HTTP request bodies or query parameters are ignored. Guard evaluation operates strictly on `AuthenticatedUserContext` derived from validated JWT signatures and server-side role resolution (`DefaultPermissionResolver`).
2. **Resource-Level Authorization (Anti-BOLA):**  
   Possessing a general permission such as `ai.conversations.create` or `ai.documents.upload` does not grant read or write access to resources owned by other users. Access attempts on peer resources return `404 Not Found` (conversations) or `403 Forbidden` (documents).
3. **Fail-Closed on Inactive Accounts:**  
   `DefaultAuthorizationEvaluator` checks `userContext.status === 'ACTIVE'`. Disabled or suspended accounts are denied immediately (403 Forbidden) before permission matching occurs.
4. **Strict Boundary Defense on Workers:**  
   Background workers dynamically verify user status via `IUserRepository` upon turn dispatch and recovery, preventing revoked users from executing queued LLM generations.
